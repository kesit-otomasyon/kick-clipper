Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

"Efsane Yayın Anları" is a private automation used by one account owner to post clips to their own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It has no public users and no sign-up.

What we access

  • TikTok: the account owner's basic profile (user.info.basic) and permission to upload videos to that same account (video.upload / video.publish).
  • YouTube: permission to upload and manage videos on the owner's channel.

What we store

Only the access tokens needed to post, kept as encrypted secrets in the owner's GitHub repository settings. We do not collect, sell or share personal data of viewers or anyone else. Public stream data (stream video and public chat counts) is used only to find moments worth clipping and is not stored after the clip is made.

Removal

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or in the Google account. Streamers can ask for any clip to be removed by opening an issue at github.com/kesit-otomasyon/kick-clipper/issues.
